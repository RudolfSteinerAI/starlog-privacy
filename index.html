STARLOG privacy policy

Package com.starlog.app · Updated 22 September 2026

STARLOG is a personal log that stays on your device. This page says what the app stores, and what it does with the microphone, camera, speech, export, and PIN.

Entries stay on the device

Log text, tags, classification, stardate, officer or vessel name, the PIN hash, audio, and video are stored in the app’s private storage on the device. There is no STARLOG account. There is no STARLOG server for logs.

STARLOG does not sell data and does not show ads. It does not upload your log entries. Android backup and device-to-device transfer of app data are turned off.

Microphone and camera

  • Microphone — used only when you tap Record Audio, Record Video, or Dictate.
  • Camera — used only when you tap Record Video.

Audio and video you record stay on the device with the log.

Speech

Dictate is optional. STARLOG uses speech only when the device reports that on-device speech is available. The app does not silently fall back to a cloud speech-to-text service.

STARLOG does not send that audio to a STARLOG server. A phone maker’s own speech service may still handle the audio. STARLOG does not control that service, and does not promise that vendor speech never leaves the phone.

Export

A JSON copy, a file export, or the system share sheet runs only when you tap it. The JSON export is text and metadata only. Audio and video stay on the device and are not included in the JSON file.

PIN

You can set an optional PIN. STARLOG stores a hash of that PIN on the device (SHA-256) and uses it as a lock. The PIN hash does not encrypt your log entries. STARLOG cannot recover a forgotten PIN.
